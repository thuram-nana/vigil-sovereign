**★★ THE DURABLE LESSONS (the most valuable output of this stream):**
1. **"The regex didn't match" ≠ "the tag isn't there." A failed parse is NEVER proof of absence** — pair every value-match with a PRESENCE probe and REFUSE when present-but-unparseable, rather than falling through and reading the WRONG tag.
2. **A fix for an assert-the-negative bug can commit the identical error one layer inward.** Re-attack the FIX's own new surface — RED-PEN's scorecard was *two rounds, two fixes, two defects introduced BY the fixes*: "that rate is the argument for the gate, not against it."
3. **Parameterize the benign twin over the axis carrying the defect — which is the one you didn't think of.** Round 1 the twin varied record CONTENT (missed domain SHAPE); round 2 it varied content+shape (missed record ENCODING). *"A twin that only varies the dimensions you already thought about cannot falsify the assumption you didn't."*
4. **Parse the CANONICAL form, not the presentation form.** A DNS TXT record is a QUOTED character-string on the wire (RFC 1035 §3.3.14 splits long ones into ADJACENT strings a resolver CONCATENATES). Normalize at a defined seam before reading any tag, and state the accepted format in the charter — "exactly as published" is the ambiguity that CAUSED the defect.
5. **Coercion upstream of a strict guard defeats it AND launders permanently** (retention happens before minting). Retain attestations by strict identity (`is True`), never `bool()`.
6. **A fired certificate must NAME the evidence it resolved** (the org domain) or a third party can't audit the claim; refuse self-contradictory evidence rather than taking whichever branch fires.
7. **★ NEAR-ZERO-FP CANNOT BE SELF-CERTIFIED BY ONE REVIEWER.** RED-PEN attested PASS and an INDEPENDENT 6-lens sweep (attack+refute-each, disjoint attack model — one skeptic vs a lens panel) then found TWO MORE CRITICAL false-positives on the SAME fault line. The dual gate is the fix; fold the sweep into a STANDING FORGE stage-9 companion. Corollary: **a guard is only as real as its REACHABILITY from the PRODUCER path** — a correct, unit-tested guard fed by a hand-built control is green while the real sensor→ingest path bypasses it (R6). Test through the real producer.
8. **When a reviewer prescribes a fix, take the SOUNDER reading and argue back.** RED-PEN said "refuse any multi-record blob"; that would have made `spf_permissive` inert on the commonest real export. RFC record SELECTION was the sharp instrument; I overrode and RED-PEN conceded. The gate is adversarial, not authoritarian.
9. **The fault line generalizes: parsing a PRESENTATION format is a lossy-transform minefield.** Every layer (record content → domain shape → encoding → cardinality → §5.1 escapes → the PRODUCER's own retention) hid one more instance. Decode to the CANONICAL/WIRE form at ONE tested seam (differential-test the decoder against an independent reference), then read semantics over wire bytes only.
