# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

VIGIL is a small project working on serious tooling. This document sets the standard for
how people behave in its spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, security
correspondence, and any other forum where the project is the subject.

It is short on purpose. The rules are not complicated; enforcing them is what matters.

## Standard

**Expected.** Be direct and be civil — the two are compatible. Criticise work, not people.
Assume competence and good faith until shown otherwise. Accept review as a normal part of
building software. Be precise about what you know, what you assume, and what you are
guessing; in this project, overclaiming is a technical failure as well as a social one.

**Not acceptable.**

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained personal hostility.
- Discriminatory or demeaning remarks about a person or a group — including on the basis of
  age, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of
  experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
  caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Threats, doxxing, or encouraging any of the above.
- Deliberately wasting maintainer time: bad-faith argument, repeated off-topic escalation,
  or reopening settled decisions without new information.
- Using project spaces to solicit or coordinate unauthorized testing of systems. VIGIL is
  authorized-use-only tooling; see [`ACCEPTABLE-USE.md`](ACCEPTABLE-USE.md). This is grounds
  for immediate removal.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces, and when an individual is representing the project in public.
It does not extend to unrelated private conduct, except where that conduct creates a
credible risk to people in the project's spaces.

## Reporting

Report a concern to the maintainer:

- **Email:** `conduct@thuramnana.com` — *placeholder: confirm this mailbox is monitored
  before publication*
- **Fallback (published maintainer address):** `thuram@thuramnana.com`, subject
  `VIGIL code of conduct`

Reports are read by the maintainer, Junior Thuram Nana, Founder & CEO of SIGIL Limited. They are handled as confidentially
as is practical; your identity is not disclosed to the person reported without your
agreement, except where the maintainer is legally required to disclose it.

**Honest limitation:** this project has a single maintainer and no independent conduct
committee. If your report concerns the maintainer, there is no internal appeal — say so in
your report and escalate to the platform hosting the discussion (for example, GitHub's own
abuse-reporting process) if that is more appropriate.

Aim: acknowledge within **3 business days**, and reach a decision within **14 days**. These
are targets, not guarantees.

To report a **security vulnerability**, use [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead — that is a
different process with different handling.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and pattern, the maintainer may: give a private correction; issue a
warning; edit or remove the offending content; temporarily restrict participation; or
permanently ban an individual from project spaces. Where a decision is made, the maintainer
states the reason to the person affected.
