VIGIL vs. Burp Suite

Burp Suite is the gold standard for hands-on web testing — an expert in the loop with a proxy, Repeater, and a deep extension ecosystem. VIGIL is autonomous: it drives Burp-class techniques at machine scale and, crucially, proves each hit with a signed, offline-verifiable exploit. Many teams run both — Burp for deep manual work, VIGIL to cover the surface and prove what's real.

Where Burp wins

Deep, interactive manual testing by an expert; the Repeater/Intruder workflow; a vast extension ecosystem; two decades of maturity. If a human is hands-on-keyboard on one target, Burp is hard to beat.

Where VIGIL wins

Autonomy and coverage at scale; a signed proof you can re-verify offline with zero VIGIL code installed; zero false positives by construction; reach beyond web into cloud, Kubernetes, source and CI; and a sovereign, BYO-LLM deployment.

FAQ

Does VIGIL replace Burp?

No — and we won't pretend it does. Burp is the best manual web-testing workbench. VIGIL covers the surface autonomously and proves what's exploitable; teams commonly run both.

Can VIGIL prove a finding Burp surfaced?

Yes. VIGIL can take a lead and either mint a signed FACT (an oracle fired) or leave it an honest LEAD — so your triage queue shrinks to things that are actually exploitable.

Is my traffic sent to a cloud?

No. VIGIL is self-hosted and air-gappable; it talks to the LLM backend you configure (including a local one). No telemetry.
