Support

How can we help?

Email info@sigilsovereign.com. A real person at SIGIL SARL reads every message, and we aim to reply within 2 business days. That is a target, not a promised response time: the Service has no service-level agreement unless one is agreed in writing (Terms of Service).

To help us help you, include the name of your workspace and what you were doing when the problem appeared. Never send us a password, a one-time code or a secret: we will never ask for one.

Report a security issue in VIGIL

Coordinated disclosure with safe harbour for good-faith research: we aim to acknowledge a report within 3 business days, and the default disclosure window is 90 days. Email info@sigilsovereign.com or open a private advisory on our repository; our security.txt has the details. Please don't file security reports in public issues.

Common questions about the hosted service

How do credits and plans work?

Everything VIGIL does is paid in credits. You can buy credit packs from $5 to $100 ($1 = 100 credits), and purchased credits never expire. A monthly plan (Starter, Pro or Team) adds a set number of credits each month; plan credits are used first and expire at the end of the month, then your purchased credits are used. If both run out, an assessment pauses safely with the results so far kept. Top up your credits, then resume it from Assessments within 7 days; if it is not resumed in time, it closes and is charged only for what it used.

I forgot my password

Choose Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen. We email you a 6-digit code and a single-use token — there is no link to click. Type the code, or paste the token on the reset page, to choose a new password. If you signed up with Google, sign in with Google instead.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

On the Plans screen. A change or a cancellation takes effect at your next renewal, and a cancelled plan runs to the end of the month you have already paid for. In the last day before a renewal the screen may refuse; if it does, write to us before the renewal date and we will stop it.

Can I get a refund?

See section 8 of the Terms of Service. If you buy as a consumer, you may have a 14-day right of withdrawal, and we refund the credits of that purchase you have not used.

Which systems can I test?

Only systems you are authorized to test. VIGIL tests a host only after you prove you control it (a DNS record, a file under /.well-known/, or a meta tag); see section 3 of the Terms of Service.

How do I delete my data or close my account?

Write to us at info@sigilsovereign.com. We answer deletion requests within 30 days: we delete the data that can be deleted and tell you what we kept and why. Our Privacy notice explains what is kept after an account closes.

Self-hosted VIGIL

If you run VIGIL on your own hardware, the operator manual ships inside the product and the deployment guides live with the source you run. Your data stays on your infrastructure, except what you send to the model backend you configure; see the Privacy notice.
