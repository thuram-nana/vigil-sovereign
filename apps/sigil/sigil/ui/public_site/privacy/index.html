Privacy

VIGIL is offered in two ways with genuinely different privacy stories: the hosted service at app.vigilguard.net, and VIGIL running on your own hardware. We describe both plainly.

The hosted service

The hosted VIGIL service is provided by SIGIL SARL (Sovereign Integrity Governance Infrastructure Labs), Buea, Cameroon, RCCM RC.BUA.2026.B.0190, under our Terms of Service. SIGIL SARL decides how your account, sign-in and billing data are used. The testing evidence, findings and reports the Service produces from your hosts are processed on your behalf, to provide the Service to you.

What we collect

  • Account data — the email address and sign-in credentials you use to create and access a workspace, and the workspace's members and their roles.
  • Hosts and testing evidence — the hosts you register, their ownership proofs, and what the Service captures while testing them. That can include raw responses from your systems and any personal data in them, and the findings and reports drawn from them.
  • Billing data — your purchases, plan, credit balance and usage statement. Card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored by us.
  • Security records — records of sign-ins and security-relevant actions, kept to protect your account and the Service.

The providers that process it

We use these providers to run the hosted service. We list only providers the hosted service actually uses:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS), eu-west-1 (Ireland) — hosting, encrypted backups, and account email (verification, receipts and security notices) through Amazon SES.
  • Cloudflare — DNS, content delivery and DDoS protection for our sites, and routing of email sent to our addresses.
  • Stripe — card payments in US dollars. Card data is processed by Stripe, not stored by us.
  • Google — sign-in, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • GitHub — only if you connect a repository for code review.
  • A third-party AI processor — the AI features. To provide them, relevant content (for example, responses from your hosts and the findings drawn from them) is sent to this processor under an API key VIGIL holds.

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, other countries, including the United States.

Cookies and scripts

The hosted service uses only the strictly-necessary session cookies listed in our Cookie notice. Our pages load no third-party scripts, and there is no analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking, so there is no consent banner.

Retention, and after your account closes

  • While your account is open, we keep your data so the Service can show it to you. It does not expire on a timer.
  • When a workspace is closed, it and its memberships are revoked and its plan stops being honoured, so it can no longer be used. Cancel any plan first (Terms, section 14) so that the payment processor does not renew it. A member who belongs to no other workspace has their account and sign-in methods revoked too.
  • The workspace's billing records (its credit ledger, invoices and usage records) are kept as append-only, signed history, so that they cannot be silently changed; no customer can read them through the Service any more. Other records of accounts and findings are kept in the same kind of ledger and cannot be deleted one by one. We also keep payment records for as long as the law requires.
  • Your other data is not deleted automatically when the workspace closes. Ask us at info@sigilsovereign.com to delete it: we will delete the data that can be deleted, and tell you what we kept and why.
  • Copies of your data remain in encrypted backups taken before it was deleted. Under our current schedule, backups on our server are removed once they are more than 30 days old, except the eight most recent; copies in our off-site backup storage are not removed automatically.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at info@sigilsovereign.com. Depending on where you are, you may have further rights, including the right to complain to a data-protection authority. If you need a data processing agreement for personal data in Customer Data (as defined in section 12 of our Terms of Service), write to info@sigilsovereign.com and we will agree one with you.

Self-hosted VIGIL

You can also run VIGIL on your own hardware with your own keys. SIGIL SARL does not receive your data in this mode:

  • Your data stays on your infrastructure, except what you send to the model backend you configure. If that backend is a cloud provider, prompt content (which can include responses from your targets and your findings) goes to that provider; see ACCEPTABLE-USE.md section 6.
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no advertising, no auto-update. There is no phone-home and every outbound path is source-audited.
  • No SIGIL SARL processor. VIGIL talks only to the LLM backend you configure, which can be a model running entirely on your own hardware.
  • Only strictly-necessary cookies for an operator/console session. No consent banner is required because there is nothing non-essential to consent to. Cookie details →
  • Erasure is by cryptographic shredding: destroy the keys, and the evidence is unrecoverable.

What we never do

We don't sell data, we don't run advertising or cross-site tracking, and we don't claim an independent audit or attestation we don't hold. See the trust center for the control details and our roadmap.
