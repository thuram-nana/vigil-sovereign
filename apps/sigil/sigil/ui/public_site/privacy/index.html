Privacy

Privacy

VIGIL has two modes with genuinely different privacy stories. We describe both plainly. This statement reflects how the software behaves; the hosted-mode terms are finalised as that offering ships.

Self-hosted / sovereign mode (the default)

You run VIGIL on your own hardware with your own keys. In this mode:

  • Your data never leaves your machine. Scan targets, findings, and evidence stay on your infrastructure.
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no advertising, no auto-update. There is no phone-home and every outbound path is source-audited and off by default.
  • No third-party processor. VIGIL talks only to the LLM backend you configure — which can be a model running entirely on your own hardware.
  • Only strictly-necessary cookies for an operator/console session. No consent banner is required because there is nothing non-essential to consent to. Cookie details →
  • Erasure is by cryptographic shredding: destroy the keys, and the evidence is unrecoverable.

Hosted mode (in development)

If you use a VIGIL-operated hosted offering, the operator acts as a processor for the limited data needed to run your account. That story is deliberately lean, and we will only ever list subprocessors that genuinely exist:

  • Account & sign-up data — the email and credentials you provide to create an organisation.
  • Payments — handled by our payment processors (Flutterwave and Stripe); card data is processed by them, not stored by us.
  • Email delivery — a transactional email transport you or we configure, for verification and recovery messages.
  • LLM provider — only the model provider you choose for hosted analysis.

Retention follows documented defaults; you can exercise access and erasure requests, satisfied through the same cryptographic-shredding mechanism. Governing-law and controller-contact details are finalised with counsel before the hosted offering is generally available.

What we never do

We don't sell data, we don't run advertising or cross-site tracking, and we don't claim an independent audit or attestation we don't hold. See the trust center for the control details and our roadmap.
