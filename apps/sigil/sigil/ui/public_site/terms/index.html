10. What results mean, and the effect of testing

  • Findings are provided as-is. No assessment finds everything. A result with no findings means only that nothing was confirmed within the coverage the result states. It is not a statement that a system is secure, compliant or free of vulnerabilities, and it is not suitable as the sole basis for a regulatory, contractual or audit decision.
  • Testing can affect your systems. Even when it works exactly as designed, testing traffic can slow or interrupt a service, create data (accounts, records, uploads, messages), trigger emails, webhooks or other side effects, and set off alerts at your providers. Prefer a staging environment where you have one.
  • Backups are your responsibility. Before you test anything you cannot afford to lose, take a backup you have actually restored from, and keep someone who can act on the system available while it is being tested.
  • Remediation is your decision. Fix guidance is a recommendation. Review it before you apply it, and test changes before they reach production.
