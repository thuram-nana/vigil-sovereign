Cookies & local data

Cookies & local data

Short version: this public site sets no cookies at all, and the product uses only strictly-necessary session cookies. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no cross-site tracking — so there's nothing to consent to, and no consent banner to click through.

This public site

These marketing, tools, and documentation pages are static. They set no cookies and load no third-party scripts. The CVSS calculator and other tools run entirely in your browser; anything they remember (if you ask them to) stays in your browser's local storage and is never sent anywhere.

The product (strictly-necessary only)

When you sign in to a VIGIL surface, a small number of session cookies are required for authentication and security. They are HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict (and Secure over HTTPS):

CookiePurposeCategory
sigil_sessionOperator/owner console sessionStrictly necessary
vigil_customer_sessionCustomer-plane session (hosted mode)Strictly necessary
OIDC binding cookieBinds a "sign in with…" flow to your browser (anti-CSRF), short-livedStrictly necessary

That's the entire list. There are no analytics, preference, or marketing cookies, so there is no cookie-preferences panel to manage — turning these off would simply mean you can't stay signed in.

Why no banner?

Consent banners exist to get permission for non-essential tracking. We don't do any, so asking would be theatre. This page is the honest disclosure instead. See privacy for the fuller picture and the trust center for the controls behind it.
