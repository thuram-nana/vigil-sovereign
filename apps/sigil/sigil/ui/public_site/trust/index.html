Trust center

Trust, by proof — not a badge wall.

Most trust pages ask you to trust a logo. VIGIL's whole design is that you don't have to: a finding re-verifies offline, controls fail closed, and every public claim traces to source and a test. Here's the honest account — including what we don't yet have.

Verify it yourself

Re-verify a finding offline — with nothing of ours installed

Every confirmed finding ships as a signed, proof-carrying certificate: the inputs, the oracle, and the observed result. A third party re-runs the check from that evidence alone and gets the same verdict. The verifier is small, auditable, and content-hash-pinned — trust the math, not our dashboard.

# re-prove a VIGIL finding from its evidence bundle, offline
python3 -m framework.v2 verify ./evidence/<finding>.json

Security controls

Fail-closed by default in production posture

Eight production gates

In production posture VIGIL refuses to start unless all hold: sealed key vault, non-permissive sovereignty, entitlement enforcement, backup + re-prove timers, a signed engagement charter, the legacy owner-token disabled, the egress supervisor armed, and a witness quorum.

Owner-key custody

The owner holds the only signing keys, with an HSM/YubiKey option and m-of-n approval for irreversible actions. The key never touches a customer-facing process.

Two-plane isolation (FATAL-2)

The key-holding governor carries no offensive capability and cannot even load offense code — a formally-stated, test-enforced boundary.

Tamper-evident history

An append-only, owner-signed event spine with anti-rollback. (Append-only and anti-rollback are live; the hard-delete/prune step is built but gated off by default.)

Sovereignty tiers

Four enforced tiers from connected to air-gapped. A tier is not, by itself, a network control — pair it with your firewall.

Zero telemetry · audited egress

No phone-home, no auto-update. The "everything else" outbound bucket is empty and CI-gated; every egress is source-audited.

Independent audit & standards — roadmap

VIGIL holds no third-party attestations today (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, PCI, FedRAMP). These are on our roadmap, and we will say so only when a report genuinely exists — never before. We make no compliance claim about VIGIL itself in the meantime.

Policies & disclosure

Security policy, privacy, terms, and acceptable-use are published and versioned. Found a flaw in VIGIL? Our coordinated-disclosure process has a 3-day acknowledgement and a 90-day window, with safe harbour for good-faith research.
